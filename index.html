How we work

Experiment first

Most of what we build starts as an experiment — a tool we wanted for ourselves. We try things, throw away what doesn't work, and keep what does.

Small by design

No feature sprawl, no growth hacks. We prefer software that does one thing well, respects your time, and stays out of your way.

Tested in private

New projects are shared with a small circle of testers before anything else. If you're curious about what we're working on, say hello.
